No picture is stored or copied. The only sound is the background music in `public/music/`, generated as above. The Hwadam walk is an impression of a real arboretum, not a copy of it: no photograph, logo or map of the place was used, and the way does not follow its real paths. The aurora scene began from one photograph its author showed as a mood: a small figure with a light under a snowy peak and a green aurora. Nothing of that photograph is in the repo, and its place was not reproduced: the ridge, the horns, the fjord, the cabin and the sky here are this demo's own, made in code.
